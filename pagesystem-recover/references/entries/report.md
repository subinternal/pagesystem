# Report (report)

Kind: system. A monthly business review: the headline measures first, then goals, composition, ranking, every measure and retention.

Task: Understand how the business did this period, what changed and where to look next.

Composition: opening ([page-header](page-header.md)) → headline ([kpi-strip](kpi-strip.md)) → goals ([goal-progress](goal-progress.md)) → composition ([breakdown](breakdown.md)) → ranking ([bar-list](bar-list.md)) → measures ([kpi-table](kpi-table.md)) → retention ([retention-cohort](retention-cohort.md)).

## content

- A title and one sentence that states what changed, in words
- Headline measures with their change against the previous period
- Goals with pace expected by today
- Where the total comes from, and the ranked contributors
- Every measure with current, previous, change and status
- Retention by cohort
- The period, the date the figures run to, and a sample label

## states

- ready
- a measure with no data says why
- a goal behind pace
- a partial current period

## responsive

- Regions keep one reading order; the composition and ranking pair sits side by side when there is room and stacks when there is not.
- Each chart follows its own narrow layout inside its region.

## checks

- One page title and ordered region headings.
- The summary sentence agrees with the figures beneath it.
- Every chart reads without script: values and a data table are in the markup.
- Each figure is derived once; no number is typed in two places.
- Text contrast and focus are measured in both themes.
