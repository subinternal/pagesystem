# CRM table (crm-table)

Kind: component. Sort, search and select accounts in one grid with owners, pipeline, win odds and recent activity.

Task: Find the accounts that need attention, compare their pipeline and odds, and act on several at once.

Elements: [text](text.md), [button](button.md), [input](input.md), [badge](badge.md).

Category: data.

Native behavior: crm-table. It needs pagesystem.js (see ../implementation.md).

## content

- Clearly labeled fictional accounts and owners
- Company, stage, owner, open deals, pipeline value, win probability, twelve-week activity and last contact per row
- Totals for the rows in view and a count of what is selected
- A plain recovery action when nothing matches
- A hidden record per account: ARR, primary contacts with role, recent touches with date and channel, the next step with owner and due date, and the stage reached for accounts off the path
- The pipeline total in its column header, following the filter

## states

- sorted by any column, ascending or descending
- searched
- filtered by stage
- some rows selected with a mixed header box
- all rows in view selected
- selection partly hidden by the filter
- marked contacted
- no results
- scrolled horizontally
- account card open
- account card on an at-risk account
- next step overdue

## responsive

- The grid grows with its rows and scrolls sideways inside a labeled region when it is wider than its panel; the checkbox and company columns stay pinned so every row stays identifiable.
- A host may cap its height with --crm-max-height; then the header and totals rows hold while rows scroll, and scrolling hands off to the page at either end.
- Search and stage share a line on a phone; the bulk action bar takes its own row.

## checks

- Exactly one column header carries aria-sort and its arrow matches the order of the rows.
- The header checkbox is mixed when only some rows in view are selected and disabled when none are in view.
- Shift-click selects the range between two rows in their current order.
- Totals and the average are computed from the rows in view only.
- Numbers are right-aligned with tabular figures; the win meter and activity line are hidden from assistive technology behind the exact value and a short trend label.
- Mark contacted updates each selected row's last contact date, clears the selection and announces the change.
- The account name is a button with aria-expanded and aria-controls; the card sits in its own full-width row after its account, and the row header's name stays the company name.
- Clicking the account again or pressing Escape closes the card and keeps focus on the account; selection checkboxes never open it.
- The card follows its account through sorting, closes when a filter hides it, and refreshes after Mark contacted.
- ARR is ranked against every account in the table; win likelihood marks the table average.
