# Agent map (agent-map)

Kind: component. An agent answer that shows the places or route it found on an authored map, with the travel mode above the map and the stops on cards.

Task: Compare the ways an agent found to get somewhere, or the places it recommends, and pick one, so the map and the list agree.

Elements: [button](button.md), [badge](badge.md), [text](text.md).

Category: agent.

Native behavior: agent-map. It needs pagesystem.js (see ../implementation.md).

## content

- The question asked and the agent answer in plain text
- An inline SVG map labeled as sample and not to scale, with streets, water, parks and a few street and area names
- A chosen route in ink and its alternative in gray, dotted for walking and solid for transit or driving
- A route summary in text naming the shown mode, duration and distance and the alternative, beside the travel-mode button and destination card that repeat them
- Start and destination pins, the destination time, and a current-location mark
- A travel-mode menu listing each mode with its time and distance
- Place cards or rows with a category icon, name, rating with review count, kind and, in the list layout, address and description
- A visible sample badge and caption
- Per place, hidden sample details: why the agent went there with its source, 2 or 3 facts and a confidence percentage; per leg, from, to, distance, travel time and why it was chosen.

## states

- settled: map, pins and cards readable with no script
- entrance: pins rise in sequence and the chosen route draws in, once
- travel mode open, with the selected mode checked
- mode changed: routes, destination time and card note updated
- place selected: card or row pressed and labeled Selected in text, matching pin marked
- selection cleared
- map expanded to a centered sheet
- reduced motion: no entrance, route drawing or menu animation
- place card open with its stop number, the legs before and after, facts and a confidence meter
- leg expanded inside a place card
- leg card open from a route line

## responsive

- Below 36rem of component width the map becomes square and the cards or recommendation list stack beneath it instead of floating over it.
- The map canvas covers its stage at no less than the drawing scale and centers on the route or places, so labels never shrink and the drawing edge never shows.
- Pins are positioned in drawing coordinates and stay aligned with the map at every width and in the expanded sheet.
- The expanded sheet keeps the map at its own proportions within the viewport; on narrow screens the stops scroll beneath the map.
- From 36rem of component width the card sits beside its pin or leg, clear of the controls, stop cards and list, narrowing or scrolling inside rather than leaving the map; below that, and always in the list layout, it opens under the chosen entry.

## checks

- Choosing a travel mode updates the routes, the route summary, the destination time on the pin, the button and the destination card together, with mode, duration and distance in text.
- The selected route and stop are named in text as well as shown by color, ring or fill.
- Selecting a card, row or pin selects the same place everywhere, scrolls the recommendation list to it and announces it in a status region.
- Arrow keys, Home and End move between places with one tab stop; Enter or Space selects; Escape closes the menu, then the expanded map, then clears the selection.
- The travel-mode menu opens from the keyboard without motion and returns focus to its button.
- Pins are pointer shortcuts hidden from assistive technology; every place stays reachable through its card or row.
- Map colors derive from the neutral roles and source hues, streets are thin flat lines with no shading, and the map holds in light and dark themes.
- Entrance and route-drawing motion stop under reduced motion, and disposal clears timers and collapses the expanded sheet.
- No map tiles, images or network requests are used; routes, ratings and times are labeled sample data.
- Choosing a place opens its card; choosing it again or pressing Escape closes it with focus kept on the place; one card is open at a time; place buttons carry aria-expanded and aria-controls and leg rows are disclosure buttons; the card never causes page overflow.
