# Breakdown (breakdown)

Kind: component. One whole split into its parts: a 100% stacked bar and a legend table that both reads and drives it.

Task: See how a total divides into its parts, how big each part is, how it changed, and which small parts were folded together.

Elements: [text](text.md), [button](button.md), [badge](badge.md).

Category: data.

Native behavior: breakdown. It needs pagesystem.js (see ../implementation.md).

## content

- A clearly labeled sample with its period
- A headline total derived from the parts, with a delta pill against the previous period
- One 100% stacked bar on a labeled 0⁠–⁠100% scale, segments separated by a gap of background
- A legend table: swatch, name, value, share and change for each part
- An Other row listing the members folded into it with their shares
- A pinned inverse tip over the highlighted segment
- A View data table with every part unfolded

## states

- no highlight
- row or segment hovered
- part chosen
- Other chosen
- two periods compared
- budget with unallocated remainder

## responsive

- The tip shares the scale row, so it never pushes the bar; under 30rem it is hidden and the legend and live status restate it.
- When the panel is narrow against the text size the value column gives way to the share; with enlarged text the legend scrolls sideways inside its own region.
- Other's members take a full-width line under its row and wrap.

## checks

- Shares are derived from the parts and add to exactly 100.0%; the total, delta and folding are derived, never typed.
- Parts under 3% fold into Other only when two or more would fold; at most six named parts keep a color, and Other is drawn in the neutral line color.
- Segments separate by a gap of background, never a stroke, and every color comes from the series tokens.
- Hovering, focusing or choosing a legend row highlights its segment, and hovering or clicking a segment highlights or chooses its row; the other segments keep their hue at a lower strength.
- Legend rows are one tab stop: arrows move, Home and End jump, Enter or Space chooses, Escape clears; a polite live status restates the choice.
- The tip is aria-hidden; the plot is a labeled image and the legend table carries every value.
- Two periods share one scale with the current period first; the change column is the shift in share in points and reads neutral, not good or bad.
- A budget draws equal-value cells allocated by largest remainder, colored by owner, with the unallocated remainder left empty and listed.
