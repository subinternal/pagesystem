# Bar list (bar-list)

Kind: component. A ranked list of names and values, each with a bar on one shared scale, its share of the total and, optionally, its change.

Task: See which items lead a measure, how far apart they are, and compare any one item with the rest.

Elements: [text](text.md), [button](button.md), [badge](badge.md).

Category: data.

Native behavior: bar-list. It needs pagesystem.js (see ../implementation.md).

## content

- A clearly labeled sample with its period
- A headline total derived from the rows, with a delta pill when a previous total is given
- Column labels for the name, value, share and optional change
- Each row's name, a bar behind it on a scale shared by every row, its value and its share of the total
- An optional change pill per row, or an initial or icon mark before the name
- A Show all control when there are more rows than the collapsed limit
- A readout that restates the chosen row and compares it with the leader
- A View data table with every row

## states

- collapsed to the top five
- expanded
- sorted by value
- sorted by name
- row chosen with the rest in context
- new row with no previous value
- compact

## responsive

- Rows share one grid, so values and shares align down the list at every width.
- Long names truncate with an ellipsis inside the bar; the full name stays in the row's accessible name and the data table.
- Under 30rem a list with a change column drops the share column; the share stays in the readout and the data table.
- The sort control wraps under the title on a narrow panel.

## checks

- Bar lengths are value over the largest value of all rows, including hidden ones, so expanding never rescales.
- Shares are derived from the values and add to exactly 100.0%; the headline total and its delta are derived, never typed.
- Rows are one tab stop: arrows move and choose, Home and End jump, Enter or Space toggles the choice, Escape clears it.
- Choosing a row deepens its bar, grays the others to the context color, draws a dashed reference line at its value through every row and tags that value at the scale's top edge; the readout restates its rank, value, share and gap to the leader.
- Each row's accessible name reads its name, value with unit, share and change in order, starting with the visible text.
- Deltas carry an arrow and a sign; color reads good or bad for the metric's direction and never carries meaning alone.
- Show all reports its state with aria-expanded and controls the list; collapsing releases a choice that becomes hidden.
- Sorting by pointer moves rows in at most 220ms; keyboard sorting and reduced motion move them at once.
