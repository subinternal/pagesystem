# Retention cohort (retention-cohort)

Kind: component. A cohort triangle: each signup cohort's retained share by period on one sequential hue, with a size-weighted average row.

Task: See whether newer signup cohorts hold on better than older ones, read any cohort at any period exactly, and compare it with the weighted average.

Elements: [text](text.md), [badge](badge.md).

Category: data.

Native behavior: retention-cohort. It needs pagesystem.js (see ../implementation.md).

## content

- A clearly labeled sample product and the cohort grain (week or month)
- A headline: the chosen period's retention for the cohort of interest, with a delta pill against the size-weighted average of the cohorts before it
- Each cohort's start, size and retained share from period 0 to the latest period it has reached
- A weighted-average row derived from the cohorts above it, never typed separately
- A stepped key for the sequential scale, drawn with the same fills as the cells
- A readout naming the cohort, the period, retained users of cohort size and the exact percentage

## states

- weekly grid
- monthly grid
- selected cell
- period in progress
- marked cohort after a product change
- curves with one chosen cohort
- negative trend

## responsive

- On a wide panel the whole triangle fits; under 36rem cells narrow and the grid scrolls sideways inside its region with the cohort column pinned.
- The headline figures wrap from one row to a stack.
- In the curves view the pinned tip is hidden under 30rem; the readout below carries the same values.
- The scale gutter and the average's edge tag grow with text size, so 200% text neither clips nor overflows.

## checks

- Cell fills come from one series hue in six steps; the two darkest steps take surface-colored text and every cell keeps 4.5:1 contrast in light and dark themes.
- Percentages, the weighted-average row and the headline are derived from retained users and cohort size in the markup.
- The grid is a single tab stop: arrows move by cell, Up and Down clamp to a shorter row's last cell, Home and End go to the row's ends, Ctrl+Home and Ctrl+End to the first and last cells.
- The selected cell's row and column headers are set in ink; the readout states cohort, period, retained users and percentage, and says when a period is still in progress.
- A delta states its direction in its arrow and sign, never by color alone.
- In the curves view other cohorts fall back to context gray, the chosen cohort takes the series color, the average is dashed with its value tagged at the right edge, and the tip follows the keys, the cohort control and the pointer.
