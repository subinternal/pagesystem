# Plan card (plan-card)

Kind: component. A plan an agent proposes, with ordered steps, step detail and an approve, edit or reject decision that belongs to the person.

Task: Review the steps an agent wants to run, open any step for its detail, then approve, edit or reject the plan before anything starts.

Elements: [text](text.md), [button](button.md), [badge](badge.md), [input](input.md).

Category: agent.

Native behavior: plan-card. It needs pagesystem.js (see ../implementation.md).

## content

- The plan's title, who proposed it, and a summary of one paragraph.
- Ordered steps, each with a status (pending, in progress, done or blocked), what the agent will do and why, an estimate, dependencies, and the files and tools it touches; done steps add duration and an output summary; blocked steps add the reason and since when.
- A decision area: approve, edit steps or reject, with a note that nothing runs until the person approves.
- Sample plans are labeled as samples.

## states

- awaiting approval
- steps collapsed to the first three
- all steps shown
- step detail card open (one at a time)
- editing steps
- edit rejected for an empty step
- approved
- rejected
- running with done, in-progress, blocked and pending steps
- stopped
- auto-approve counting down
- auto-approve paused while the person reviews
- auto-approve stopped
- approved automatically

## responsive

- At narrow widths the status badge moves under the title and the actions fill the row, with Approve on its own line.
- Step rows wrap long text beside their status mark; the keyboard hint is hidden on touch input.
- Keep essential text at body size and controls at the house control height under text enlargement.

## checks

- Nothing runs until the person approves; an agent cannot approve its own plan.
- Every status has a text label as well as a mark; pending labels are kept for assistive technology.
- Ctrl or Command with Enter approves only while the plan awaits approval; Escape cancels an edit.
- Saving an edit with an empty step is refused, the field is marked invalid and focused.
- After each decision focus moves to the next available action and a status message announces the result.
- Auto-approve shows its remaining time, pauses while the pointer or focus is inside the card, can be stopped, and its timer is cleared on dispose.
- Reduced motion stops the in-progress mark from turning and the countdown ring from animating.
- One step card is open at a time; choosing the open step again or pressing Escape closes it and focus stays on the step.
- A blocked step's card shows its reason first.
