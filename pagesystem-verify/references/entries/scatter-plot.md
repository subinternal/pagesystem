# Scatter plot (scatter-plot)

Kind: component. Every item placed by two measures, split at the medians, with the focused or matching items in color and the rest in context gray.

Task: See how items such as accounts spread across two measures, which quadrant each falls in, and find a named item quickly.

Elements: [text](text.md), [input](input.md).

Category: data.

Native behavior: scatter-plot. It needs pagesystem.js (see ../implementation.md).

## content

- A clearly labeled sample set, the two measures and their units
- Headline figures derived from the points: the count in the leading quadrant and both medians
- Small points on a value scale with gridlines only at labeled values and the zero line in baseline ink
- Optional median lines with their values tagged at the scale edges and quadrant names in the corners
- A search field with a live count of matches
- A readout naming the focused point and a data table of every point

## states

- focused point
- search matches
- no matches
- size encoding
- labeled outliers
- categories

## responsive

- Tick counts follow the plot's width and height; the last x label carries the unit and labels that would touch are hidden.
- Quadrant names wrap inside their corner rather than collide.
- Under 30rem the plot shortens and the pinned tip is hidden; the readout carries the focused point.
- The scale gutter and the plot height grow with text size, so 200% text neither clips nor overflows.

## checks

- Points, medians, quadrant counts and outliers are derived from the data table.
- The plot is a listbox with one tab stop; arrows step through points in x order, Home and End go to the ends, and while a search is active the keys step through matches only.
- The focused point is enlarged and ringed in ink, its tip names it beside the mark, and the readout restates it with its quadrant.
- A search colors its matches, moves focus to the first one when the current point does not match, and announces the count or that nothing matches.
- Size encoding uses three steps explained in a key drawn with the same marks; categories use at most four series colors, each named at its cluster.
- Outliers lie beyond 1.5 interquartile ranges on either measure and are named where they sit.
