# Goal progress (goal-progress)

Kind: component. Progress against a set of goals: a unit meter summary, then each goal's bullet bar with its target, the pace expected by today and a status in words.

Task: See how many goals are on pace, find the ones behind, and read what each needs from here to finish.

Elements: [text](text.md), [button](button.md), [input](input.md), [badge](badge.md).

Category: data.

Native behavior: goal-progress. It needs pagesystem.js (see ../implementation.md).

## content

- The plan or budget name and how far through the period it is
- A summary count and a unit meter of small cells, one per goal or one share of a budget
- Each goal's name and owner, its actual against its target, and a status in words with its icon
- A bullet bar per goal on one shared percent-of-target scale: the value reached, a target tick and a faint pace marker
- A readout for the chosen goal: expected by today, the gap to pace and what is needed each week
- The values in a data table under View data, and a sample data label

## states

- ahead
- on pace
- behind
- complete
- goal chosen
- ordered by plan
- ordered by furthest behind
- budget: under pace, on pace, over pace and over limit
- single goal
- period closed

## responsive

- On a wide panel name, bar, figure and status share a row with a shared axis above the bars; under 36rem each row stacks name and status over a full-width bar and its figure.
- A budget meter of forty cells wraps to two rows of twenty on a narrow panel.
- Axis labels anchor the target to its tick, so they stay inside the panel at any width.

## checks

- Status is derived from progress over the elapsed share of the period: 8% or more ahead of pace is Ahead, more than 8% short is Behind, reaching the target is Complete; a budget reads the other way.
- Every bar shares one scale, so target ticks and pace markers align down the list.
- The summary count, cells and tally are derived from the statuses; cells on track come first.
- Goals are one tab stop: Up and Down move and choose, Home and End jump, and choosing a goal outlines its cell in the meter.
- Reordering keeps each row's node, so the chosen goal and focus survive; rows slide only after a pointer change and never with reduced motion.
- Status is never carried by color alone: every status has a word and an icon.
