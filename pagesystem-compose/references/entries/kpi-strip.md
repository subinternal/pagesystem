# KPI strip (kpi-strip)

Kind: component. Three to five measures on one shared period, with the chosen measure's trend drawn against the previous period below.

Task: Compare a few headline measures for the same week, month or quarter, then choose one to see how this period tracked against the last.

Elements: [text](text.md), [button](button.md), [input](input.md), [badge](badge.md).

Category: data.

Native behavior: kpi-strip. It needs pagesystem.js (see ../implementation.md).

## content

- A period selector (week, month or quarter) shared by every measure
- Each measure's caption, figure for the period and delta against the previous period
- The chosen measure's trend: this period as a solid line with a faint area, the previous period dashed, a key that draws both marks as the plot does
- A value scale at labeled round values, printing its floor because it need not start at zero, and terse date labels
- A pinned tip and a readout restating the inspected point and the same point in the previous period
- Optionally, each measure's progress to its period goal and a dashed goal reference line tagged at the scale's edge
- A measure with no data shows a dash and the reason, never a zero
- The chosen measure's values in a data table under View data, and a sample data label

## states

- week
- month
- quarter, drawn by week
- measure chosen
- point inspected
- with goals
- measure without data: a dash and a reason in the strip, a message in place of the chart

## responsive

- Measures sit in one row with dividers; under 40rem they collapse to a 2 by 2 grid and an odd last measure spans the row; under 22rem they stack.
- The pinned tip hides on a narrow panel; the readout carries the same values.
- The value gutter and date thinning scale with the text size, so labels never collide at 200% text.

## checks

- Every figure, delta, line and table row is derived from one daily history per measure; the previous period is the same number of days immediately before.
- A sum measure totals its days and a mean measure averages them; a quarter is thirteen weekly buckets.
- The measures are tabs controlling one panel: Left and Right move and choose, Home and End jump, and only the chosen tab is in the tab order.
- The chart is a slider: arrow keys, Home and End move the inspected point; its value text matches the readout.
- Changing the period updates every measure, the key, the table headings and the chart together.
- A goal reference for a sum measure is the goal pace per point; for a mean measure it is the goal itself.
