# Histogram range (histogram-range)

Kind: component. A distribution of many bands with a range brush beneath it; bands inside the range take color and the headline counts them.

Task: See how values such as order totals or response times are distributed, choose a range and know how many fall inside it and what share that is.

Elements: [text](text.md), [input](input.md).

Category: data.

Native behavior: histogram-range. It needs pagesystem.js (see ../implementation.md).

## content

- A clearly labeled sample population, its measure and the band width
- A headline: the count in range, its share of the total and the range itself
- One bar per band on a count scale that starts at zero, with the unit named once
- A brush on the same x scale with two handles and the brushed window
- Optional percentile markers and a reference value, dashed, with their values tagged above the plot and repeated in the figures
- A readout for the inspected band and a data table of every band

## states

- default range
- full range
- range at the objective
- inspected band
- percentile markers
- narrow panel

## responsive

- Edge labels step by a round multiple of the band width and thin by the space they have; the last label carries the unit and always stays.
- Marker tags that would collide move to a second row, and a tag near an edge slides inward to stay inside the figure.
- Under 30rem the plot shortens and the pinned tip is hidden; the readout carries the inspected band.
- The scale gutter grows with text size, so 200% text neither clips nor overflows.

## checks

- Bands, counts, totals and percentiles are read from the data table; the headline and the figures are derived, never typed separately.
- Each handle is a slider with its value, bounds and a value text that states the count and share in range; arrows step one band, Shift+arrows and Page keys ten, Home and End go to the limit, and the handles cannot cross.
- Pressing or dragging a handle, or pressing the track, moves the nearer handle to the nearest band edge.
- Bands inside the range use the series color and bands outside use context gray; the inspected band darkens and the tip restates it beside its bar.
- Percentiles are interpolated within their band and match the figures exactly.
- A key press or reduced motion moves the range with no transition.
